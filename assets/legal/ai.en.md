# Before you use the assistant

The assistant uses the artificial intelligence of the provider you choose, with your own key. To answer you, it sends:

- what you write in the chat;
- the store data it looks up: orders (products, totals, status, notes, the recipient's name and shipping address), products, the status of printers and the app, and the weekly report;
- if you turn on screen control, screenshots during the tasks you approve.

**Never sent:** your customers' email or phone, or your keys.

The provider handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy. AI can make mistakes: purchases and changes always ask for your confirmation. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Assistant.
